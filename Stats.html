Fireball Spell Stats

Casting Time

1 action

Range

150 feet

Target

A point you choose within range

Components

Verbal (You sayhow can i Fireball)

Somantic (You gotta point with your hands)

Material (A tiny ball of bat guano and sulfur)

Duration

Instantaneous

Available to the Classes

Sorceror, Wizard, Spellblade, and ect for some subclasses

Description

The point that you indicate using your tool or hand spawns a bright flashing light of red, yellow, and orange and it flys out towards the target in range and once it makes contact a massive, firey, explosion occurs. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on the point must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. Then take in account the damage. All of the objects that could be caught on fire are now on fire within the range of the explosion

Damage

8d6 or half as much if they succeed the save
8d6+1d6 per level after 3rd
